HR Policy

The Human Resources team helps employees understand workplace policies, employment processes, benefits, development, and respectful workplace expectations. HR is a source of guidance and a confidential place to raise a concern, but not every conversation is automatically confidential. HR explains the level of confidentiality that can be provided and may need to act when safety, legal, or serious policy issues are involved.

New employees complete onboarding tasks through the HR portal. These tasks may include providing employment information, reviewing policies, completing required training, and selecting eligible benefits. Employees should read official policy updates and keep their personal and emergency contact information current. If an employee cannot access the portal, HR can provide an approved alternative process.

The company expects a workplace free from discrimination, harassment, retaliation, and bullying. An employee who experiences or witnesses a concern should report it to their manager, HR, or another approved reporting channel. Reports are handled promptly and fairly. Employees should preserve relevant facts and messages, avoid public speculation, and cooperate honestly with an investigation. Retaliation against someone who raises a good-faith concern or participates in a review is not acceptable.

Managers have a duty to listen respectfully, avoid promising a particular outcome, and escalate concerns to HR when required. They should keep sensitive information limited to people who need it for a legitimate work purpose. Employees should not use informal group messages to investigate a complaint or share another person's personal information.

Performance feedback is an ongoing responsibility shared by employees and managers. Goals should be clear, progress should be discussed regularly, and development needs should be recorded through the company's review process. HR can explain the process and help resolve questions about eligibility or timing. The current HR portal and published policy library are the official sources for forms, deadlines, and policy versions. When an informal statement conflicts with an official policy, employees should ask HR for clarification before acting.

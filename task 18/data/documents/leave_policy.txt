Leave Policy

The company supports planned time away from work so employees can rest, handle personal responsibilities, and return ready to contribute. Employees may apply for casual leave, planned vacation, or other eligible leave through the HR portal. The request should include the dates, the type of leave, and a short note when the policy requires one.

Leave requests should normally be submitted before the planned leave date. Early notice gives the manager time to plan work coverage and helps the team avoid last-minute disruption. In an unexpected emergency, the employee should contact the manager as soon as possible and submit the HR portal request when practical. A request is not approved merely because it has been submitted; the employee should wait for the approval status before treating the leave as confirmed.

Managers are responsible for reviewing and approving leave requests. They consider staffing needs, deadlines, previous approved leave, and the employee's available balance. Managers should respond promptly and explain any rejection or request for clarification. Employees should not pressure a manager to approve leave that would create an unsafe or unreasonable staffing gap.

The HR team maintains the official leave balance and can help when an employee sees an incorrect balance, cannot access the portal, or is unsure which leave category applies. HR may request supporting documents for leave types that require them. Employees should keep their contact details current so HR can reach them about a request.

Before leave begins, employees should update their calendar, document important handoffs, and tell relevant teammates how urgent work will be covered. For an absence longer than a few days, the employee and manager should agree on a handoff plan. On return, the employee should review updates and close any pending actions. Unused leave is handled according to the employment agreement and applicable company rules. Questions about eligibility, carryover, or special leave should be directed to HR rather than guessed from an informal conversation.

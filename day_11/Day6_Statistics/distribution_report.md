# Distribution Report

## Observations

### Normal Distribution
The student heights data appears approximately normal. The values are centered around a single middle value, and the distribution is balanced around the mean. In a normal distribution, the mean, median, and mode are usually close to one another.

### Right-Skewed Distribution
The employee salary data is right-skewed. Most salaries are moderate, but a small number of very high salaries pull the mean to the right. This creates a long tail on the right side of the distribution.

### Key Insight
- For normal distribution: the data is balanced and symmetric.
- For right-skewed data: the mean is pulled upward by large values.
- In skewed data, the median is often a better measure of the center than the mean.

This tells us that distribution shape matters when interpreting averages and selecting summary statistics.

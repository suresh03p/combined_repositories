Salary Policy

The company pays employees through the payroll system on the published monthly payroll schedule. The exact pay date may move when a scheduled date falls on a bank holiday or weekend; Payroll communicates any change before the affected pay run. Employees should review their offer letter and local employment terms for the currency, pay frequency, and deductions that apply to them.

Salary is processed monthly after Payroll completes attendance, approved absence, tax, benefit, and other required checks. Managers must approve relevant time records by the cutoff shown in the payroll calendar. A late approval can delay the correction of an item to a later pay run, so employees should review their records before the cutoff and raise errors promptly.

The payslip is available through the employee payroll portal after processing. It shows gross salary, required deductions, benefits or adjustments, and net pay. Employees should protect their payroll login and should not send a full payslip through an unsecured public channel. Payroll can provide a secure route for questions about a payslip.

Employees should notify HR and Payroll promptly when their bank account, address, tax information, legal name, or other payroll details change. A change submitted after the payroll cutoff may take effect in the following month. Payroll will not change a bank account solely from an unverified message; identity and account verification protect employees from payment fraud.

Questions about missing salary, an unexpected deduction, or an incorrect amount should be submitted through the payroll support channel with the relevant pay period and a clear description. Payroll investigates the records and explains the outcome. Managers should not promise a payment date or alter payroll records themselves. Compensation reviews and promotions follow separate processes and do not automatically change the monthly payroll schedule. The HR team can explain where to find those processes, while Payroll handles pay-run questions.
